## Detailed description

**Requires a one-time $10 license key, purchased separately at
[rithmada.gumroad.com/l/fourthandgo](https://rithmada.gumroad.com/l/fourthandgo).
No free tier — this extension is fully locked until a valid key is entered.**

4th&Go is a live draft cockpit for Sleeper fantasy football drafts. Import
your own rankings — or build them from scratch in the built-in Rankings
Creator — and watch players get crossed off automatically as picks come in,
no manual tracking needed.

**What you get:**
- Live sync to any Sleeper draft (real or mock), auto-detected from an open
  Sleeper tab
- A tiered board blending as many ranking sources as you import, with live
  ADP columns and a value bar showing market discounts/reaches
- BEER/VBD value — replacement-level player value, recalculated live as the
  draft goes
- Best Picks Right Now, team position-need tracking, full roster review
- A drag-and-drop Rankings Creator to build and tier your own board by hand
- Optional draft-write actions (queue, one-click draft) through your own
  Sleeper session — off by default

**Built for a specific league shape**: 10-team, full PPR,
1QB/2RB/2WR/1TE/2FLEX, no K/DST.

**License**: a single $10 purchase unlocks the extension for as long as you
use it — no subscription, no recurring charge. Purchase and license-key
delivery are handled by Gumroad.

**Privacy**: no accounts, no tracking, no ads. See the extension's privacy
policy for exactly what's read/sent and to whom.
